Surfskates

What are surfskates?

A surfskate is a specially designed skateboard that lets you experience the feeling of surfing on the street. Thanks to its unique front truck system, it feels just like riding a wave. The big advantage? You hardly need to push to build up speed.

Surfskates are perfect for surfers who want to train on flat days, but also for anyone who enjoys a playful and smooth riding style. They help you improve your balance, control, and surfing technique.

A little history of surfskating

The origins of surfskating go back to the 1950s, when surfers were looking for a way to recreate the surfing feeling on land during calm, wave-less days. What started as a creative solution eventually grew into its own boardsport.

Today, surfskating has become an established part of surf culture worldwide.
