About us

We live for the ocean, the waves, and the laid-back surf vibe. Our place was born from a love for surfing and the beach – a spot where surfers, beach lovers, and friends come together.

In our surf shop you’ll find everything you need for a day on the water, from surfboards, surfskates, wetsuits to surfessentials

And through our activities, we bring people together on the water: surf sessions, ocean experiences, and moments that connect you to the sea.

Enjoy a small snack and a refreshing drink after your time in the water.

“Our surfclub isn’t just a vibe — it’s a reminder.”

We support ocean conservation and seahorse protection, because a healthy ocean means better waves, stronger ecosystems and a future for all sea life.
