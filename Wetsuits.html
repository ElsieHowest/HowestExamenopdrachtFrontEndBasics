Men

Performance wetsuits built for warmth, flexibility and durability.

The right wetsuit thickness depends on the water temperature, season and your cold tolerance. Thicker wetsuits provide more warmth, while thinner suits offer more flexibility.

Thickness Water temperature Best for
3/2mm 15-20°C Spring & summer surfing
4/3 10-15°C Cold water & most european surf
5/4 5-10°C Winter surfing

Tip: If you get cold easily, choose a slightly thicker wetsuit.

What do you wear under a wetsuit?

Some surfers wear swimwear under their wetsuit, while others prefer nothing underneath for a more natural fit and extra comfort.

The best choice depends on personal preference, water temperature and the fit of your wetsuit.
